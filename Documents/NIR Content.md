About Page
Description: 
Option 1: The National Innovation Repository (NIR) is a centralized digital platform that collects, preserves, manages, and showcases innovation-related knowledge, solutions, research outputs, technologies, intellectual assets, and best practices developed across Bangladesh. The repository serves as a national knowledge-sharing ecosystem, connecting innovators, academia, industry, government, and development partners to foster collaboration, technology adoption, commercialization, and innovation-driven development.
Option 2: The National Innovation Repository provides a single, trusted platform for discovering, preserving, and sharing innovations developed across Bangladesh. By connecting innovators, institutions, and knowledge, it reduces duplication of effort, promotes collaboration, supports technology commercialization, and enables policymakers, researchers, industries, and development partners to make informed decisions based on reliable innovation data. 
Vision: 
To build a connected, collaborative, and innovation-driven Bangladesh where knowledge, technology, and innovative solutions are accessible to all and contribute to sustainable national development.
Mission: 
To strengthen Bangladesh's innovation ecosystem by connecting innovators, institutions, and innovation-related knowledge through collaboration among government, academia, industry, development partners, and civil society.
Objectives: 
The National Innovation Repository (NIR) is a unified and inclusive digital platform to document, recognize, and showcase innovations across Bangladesh. It focuses on visibility, attribution, and connectivity for innovations from individuals, universities, startups, and public institutions. The system will enable collaboration among innovators, government, academia, and investors. NIR will serve as a national infrastructure for inclusive, insight-driven innovation development.
Key objectives include:
?      Reduce innovation redundancy by improving visibility and documentation.
?      Enable innovation upscaling through institutional linkage and exposure.
?      Reduce duplication of funding by identifying previously supported innovations.
?      Promote innovation diffusion across sectors, regions, and communities.
?      Showcase impactful innovations to attract partnerships, funding, or adoption.
?  Strengthen collaboration among universities, startups, government, funders and industries.
?      Support evidence-informed planning and investment in innovation.

A. News and event 
a. Pilot Evaluation of Water Innovation Challenge 2021 Projects Completed
Aspire to Innovate (a2i), ICT Division, has completed the pilot evaluation of two winning innovations WaterWise and PANI developed under the Water Innovation Challenge 2021. Both teams successfully completed their project activities and submitted their Project Completion Letters. The solutions are currently being piloted in Uttara (WaterWise) and Savar (PANI).
To assess the effectiveness of the innovations in real-world conditions and verify the final project deliverables, a field evaluation and expert review workshop was held on 19 August 2025. During the workshop, both teams presented the progress and outcomes of their pilot implementations at their respective sites.
The evaluation was conducted by a panel of experts comprising Mr. Md. Abdul Majid, Supervising Engineer and Project Director, Water Supply (Eastern & Northern) Circle; Mr. Md. Mahbubur Rahman Khan, Assistant Director (Environmental Clearance), Department of Environment; and Mr. M. D. Refat Jani, Manager, Automation & IoT, Saffix IT Limited. The experts reviewed each project's deliverables and provided recommendations for further improvement.
b. Smart Union, Upazila and Municipality Management System Successfully Evaluated in Panchagarh
Aspire to Innovate (a2i), ICT Division, has completed the evaluation of the Smart Union, Upazila and Municipality Management System, the winning innovation under the Smart District Innovation Challenge 2023. The initiative, proposed by the Panchagarh District Administration during the 2023 Deputy Commissioners' Conference, was implemented with support from the a2i Innovation Fund and has successfully completed its pilot implementation in Panchagarh.
To assess the project's performance and explore opportunities for future partnership and scale-up, a day-long evaluation workshop was held on 24 September 2025 at the Conference Room of the Panchagarh Deputy Commissioner's Office. The workshop brought together experts from the Local Government Division, technology sector, and a2i to review the pilot implementation and provide recommendations.
The evaluation panel included Mr. Md. Zakir Hossain, Senior Assistant Secretary, Local Government Division; Mr. Monjur Ahmed, Senior Cloud Architect. 
c. Smart School Bus Pilot Successfully Evaluated Under the Smart District Innovation Challenge 2023
Aspire to Innovate (a2i), ICT Division, has completed the evaluation of the Smart School Bus initiative, the winning innovation from Chattogram under the Smart District Innovation Challenge 2023. Supported through the a2i Innovation Fund, the project successfully implemented and piloted an IoT-enabled smart transportation system designed to improve the safety, security, and monitoring of school bus services.
To evaluate the pilot implementation and determine opportunities for future partnership and scale-up, a district-level evaluation workshop was held on 3 September 2025 at the Conference Room of the Chattogram Deputy Commissioner's Office. The workshop brought together experts from the education, transport, and technology sectors, along with representatives from a2i.
The evaluation panel included Md. Zobayer, System Analyst, Secondary and Higher Education Division; Md. Zulfikar Ali, Unit Head, BRTC Chattogram Bus Depot; Mr. Toufiqur Rahman, Innovation Expert, a2i; Kashfia Hasan, Young Professional, a2i; and S. M. Ishtiaq Ahmed Alvi, Young Professional, a2i.
The pilot introduced several smart technologies, including IoT-based attendance tracking, GPS-enabled real-time bus tracking, live video streaming, NFC-enabled student attendance, and instant SMS notifications. Parents received real-time updates, including photographs of students boarding and leaving the bus, live bus location, and estimated arrival times through a mobile application.
The pilot received positive feedback from both schools and parents. The experts observed that the solution significantly enhanced parents' confidence by providing reliable, real-time information about their children's transportation. Features such as live video streaming and GPS tracking further strengthened student safety and improved communication between schools and parents.
d. a2i Organizes Workshop on Creative Problem-Solving to Strengthen Citizen-Centric Innovation
Aspire to Innovate (a2i), ICT Division, organized a day-long workshop titled "From Challenges to Innovative Solutions" on 17 September 2025 at the National Museum of Science & Technology. The workshop was conducted by the a2i Innovation Cluster to strengthen creative problem-solving and design thinking in the development and delivery of citizen services.
The workshop brought together members of ongoing citizen service initiatives to explore innovative approaches to addressing operational challenges and improving public service delivery. Through interactive discussions and collaborative exercises, participants identified practical solutions and shared ideas for enhancing service efficiency and effectiveness.
The sessions focused on promoting innovation within the workplace by encouraging participants to rethink existing processes, embrace collaborative decision-making, and apply design thinking principles to real-world challenges. The workshop also provided a platform for exchanging experiences and strengthening participants' capacity to develop user-centered solutions.
