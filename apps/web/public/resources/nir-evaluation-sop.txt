NIR Evaluation Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) — v1.0

1. Institutional Coordinators triage new submissions and assign them to
   Expert Evaluators based on domain.
2. Evaluators score each submission against the standard rubric and may
   request blind (anonymized) review for sensitive categories.
3. Innovations may be Shortlisted, Rejected, or sent back for revision.
4. Shortlisted innovations proceed to a Selected decision, and Selected
   innovations are Published to the public repository.
5. All decisions and scores are recorded in the audit trail.

Aspire to Innovate (a2i), ICT Division, Government of Bangladesh.
