NIR Submission Guideline (v1.0)

This guideline explains how to prepare and submit an innovation to the
National Innovation Repository (NIR):

1. Register as an innovator and complete your profile.
2. Prepare a clear problem statement and proposed solution (plain language).
3. Attach supporting evidence: photos, a short video, and any documents
   (concept notes, pilot reports, publications).
4. Select the most specific category and, where relevant, the SDGs your
   work contributes to.
5. Submit for review. You can track status from your dashboard at any time.

Aspire to Innovate (a2i), ICT Division, Government of Bangladesh.
